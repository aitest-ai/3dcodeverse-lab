Do VRoid Studio's sample models come with conditions of use?

Both base models and sample models come with their own conditions of use, but their conditions might differ depending on the model.

Before you start editing or using a model, please make sure to take a look at their conditions of use. While some models have CC0 licenses, some others have their own particular set of conditions.

If this is your first attempt at creating 3D characters, it might be easier to pick a sample model and try editing it first. You can try changing some random parameters, textures, and colors to see how they reflect in real time. Once you get the grasp of how things work, step up your game and try designing a new hairstyle by yourself. All the textures in the .vroid files for our sample models are completely customizable. There is literally no limit to what you can do.

Sample models are available on VRoid Hub too.
You can ❤ models on VRoid Hub to call them up on integrated apps, download their VRM files, and use them on any AR/VR platform, or even use them to create brand-new 3D content.

Sample models are meant primarily for beginners and people new to the 3D creative scene. In order to leave more freedom to each user, we’re deliberately making them customizable.
Please refrain from doing anything that might harm or disturb other people.
